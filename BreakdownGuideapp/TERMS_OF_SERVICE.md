## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

### 2.1 Service Overview
Go BARRY is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed exclusively for real-time breakdown management, diagnostic assessment, and fleet operations coordination in the bus and coach transport sector. The Service enables authorised supervisors to:

- Record and track vehicle breakdowns in real-time
- Access diagnostic decision-support wizards
- Coordinate engineer dispatch and assignment
- Track replacement vehicle deployment and BSOG mileage
- Monitor route status and service disruptions
- Generate operational analytics and reports
