   ✅ What: Real-time Green/Amber/Red status for all 231 routes

   ✅ Time saved: ~3 minutes per breakdown lookup (estimated 10+ per day)
   ✅ Effort: 12-16 hours development

   ✅ What: Identify which routes have backup vehicle coverage
   ✅ Benefit: Prevents cascading failures (1 vehicle breaks down → route stops)
   ✅ Impact: 15-20% reduction in downtime on critical routes
   ✅ Effort: 14-18 hours development
   ✅ Risk: LOW

3. STOP-LEVEL INCIDENT HEATMAP
   ✅ What: Visual map showing where breakdowns cluster
   ✅ Benefit: Identifies problem areas (potholes, bad intersections) needing maintenance
   ✅ Impact: Prevents repeat breakdowns at problem locations
   ✅ Effort: 16-20 hours development
   ✅ Risk: LOW

TOTAL DEVELOPMENT EFFORT: 42-54 hours across 4-6 weeks
