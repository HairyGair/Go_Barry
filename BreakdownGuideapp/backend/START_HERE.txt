THE FIX
=======

The gobarry-backend.zip includes corrected route ordering in supervisors.js:

  BEFORE (BROKEN):
    /:id route was FIRST (catches everything including "stats")
    /:id/stats route was SECOND (never reached)

  AFTER (FIXED):
    /:id/stats route is FIRST (specific routes first)
    /:id route is LAST (generic catch-all last)

This ensures /api/supervisors/AG003/stats returns statistics, not the
supervisor object.
