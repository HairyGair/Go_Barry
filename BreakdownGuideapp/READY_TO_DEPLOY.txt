✅ Backend Endpoint
   - Route: POST /api/breakdowns/smart-route-match
   - Function: Finds bus routes within 1km of coordinates
   - Uses: GTFS database (gtfs_routes, gtfs_stops, gtfs_trips, gtfs_stop_times)
   - Returns: List of affected routes with trip counts and distances

KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

1. AUTOMATIC ROUTE SUGGESTIONS
   - When supervisor enters location, system suggests nearby routes
   - Uses real GTFS data (bus routes, stops, schedules)
   - Shows up to 5+ routes within 1km radius

2. SMART RANKING
   - Routes sorted by frequency (most trips first)
   - Shows distance to nearest stop
