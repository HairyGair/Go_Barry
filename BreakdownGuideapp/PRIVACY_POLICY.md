## 3. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

### 3.1 Cookies Used
The Service uses the following cookies:

**Essential/Functional Cookies:**
- **JWT Authentication Token** - Stores your session authentication. Purpose: To keep you logged in and authenticate requests. Duration: 24 hours. Type: HTTP-only (not accessible to JavaScript, protecting against XSS attacks)
- **CSRF Token** - Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks. Purpose: To verify legitimate requests. Duration: Session. Type: HTTP-only
- **User Preferences** - Stores your UI preferences (theme, layout). Purpose: To provide personalised experience. Duration: 1 year (unless cleared)

**Analytics Cookies:**
- We may use [Google Analytics / similar] to understand how the Service is used. This may set cookies for tracking purposes. See Section 8.2 for details about Google Analytics.

### 3.2 Cookie Consent
By using the Service, you consent to the use of cookies as described above. Essential cookies are necessary for the Service to function and cannot be disabled. You may disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings, but this may affect the Service's functionality.

### 3.3 Disabling Cookies
You can control cookie settings via your browser preferences:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

Note: Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in or using the Service.

### 6.1 Retention Periods

**Account Information:**
- Active accounts: Retained for the duration of your employment or the Customer's subscription
- Inactive accounts: Retained for 12 months after the account is closed
- After 12 months: Securely deleted unless we have a legal obligation to retain

- After retention period: Securely deleted

- After 12 months: Deleted unless required for audit or legal purposes

**Cookie Data:**

- CSRF tokens: Session duration only
- User preferences: 1 year (or until deleted)

### 6.2 Retention Exceptions
We may retain personal data beyond the above periods if:
- Required by law (e.g., tax records, legal holds)
- Necessary to defend legal claims
- Required for transport safety or regulatory compliance
- Required to prevent fraud or security threats

### 6.3 Right to Erasure (Deletion)
You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. We will delete data within 30 days unless:
- We have a legal obligation to retain it

## 9. DATA SECURITY

### 9.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect personal data:

**Technical Controls:**
- **Encryption in Transit:** TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS) for all data transmitted over the internet
- **Encryption at Rest:** Database encryption for stored personal data
- **Password Protection:** Passwords hashed using bcrypt (16+ rounds) — we never store plaintext passwords
- **Authentication:** JWT token-based authentication for all API requests
- **Authorization:** Role-based access controls (RBAC) limiting access to personal data
- **Firewalls:** Network firewalls protecting the infrastructure
- **Regular Security Updates:** Patching of security vulnerabilities as soon as available

**Organisational Controls:**
- **Access Restrictions:** Only authorised personnel have access to personal data
- **Confidentiality Agreements:** All staff sign confidentiality obligations
- **Training:** Regular data protection and security training for staff
- **Audit Logs:** Comprehensive logging of all data access and modifications
- **Backup and Disaster Recovery:** Regular backups to prevent data loss
- **Incident Response Plan:** Documented procedures for responding to security breaches

### 9.2 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting personal data:

1. **Assessment:** We will assess the type and extent of the breach within 24 hours
2. **Your Notification:** If there is a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you via email within 72 hours (or as required by law)
3. **ICO Notification:** If required by UK GDPR, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
4. **Your Rights:** You can report the breach to the ICO at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

Our Breach Response Plan includes:
- Immediate containment of the breach
- Investigation of the cause
- Remediation and prevention of recurrence
- Transparency with affected individuals
- Cooperation with regulators

### 9.3 Security Limitations
While we use reasonable security measures, no system is completely secure. You acknowledge that:
- Internet transmission is inherently insecure
- We cannot guarantee absolute security against sophisticated attacks
- You are responsible for protecting your login credentials
