How It Works

From call to resolution.

A breakdown comes in. Here's what happens next.

Driver calls in

Supervisor opens Go BARRY and starts a new breakdown. Fleet number, location, and GPS are captured immediately. Smart route matching suggests affected services.

Guided diagnosis

The diagnostic wizard walks through a structured assessment. Consistent questions, consistent outcomes - no matter who's on shift. The system recommends whether the vehicle can continue or needs recovery.

Engineer dispatched

Select an on-shift engineer. The system calculates road distance and ETA automatically. A live countdown appears on engineering and operations dashboards.

Replacement sent

If needed, dispatch a replacement from the nearest depot. Dead mileage is calculated via road distance for BSOG reporting. Return-to-service tracking completes the picture.

Live dashboards update

Every breakdown pushes to Operations, Engineering, and depot Display screens in real-time via WebSocket. Engineer ETA counts down live. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Resolved & reported

Mark the breakdown resolved when the vehicle is back in service. The full timeline is logged to the activity feed. Analytics and KPIs update automatically.
