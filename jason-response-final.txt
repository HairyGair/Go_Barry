## Response to Jason Hamilton

Hi Jason,

Found and fixed the issue! You were absolutely right - the problem was on our end.

**What was happening:**
Our Express server was applying JSON parsing middleware globally, which consumed the request body before it reached our webhook handler. This left an empty object `{}` for the handler to process.

**The fix:**
1. Modified our server to skip JSON parsing specifically for `/api/streetmanager/webhook`
2. Updated the webhook handler to read the raw body directly
3. Now properly accesses SNS fields at root level: `message.Type`, `message.SubscribeURL`, etc.

**Result:**
The webhook now correctly receives and processes SNS messages exactly as you described in your schema.

Thank you for your patience and the clear guidance. The webhook at https://go-barry.onrender.com/api/streetmanager/webhook is now ready to receive StreetManager notifications.
