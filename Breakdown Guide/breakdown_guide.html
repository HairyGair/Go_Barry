Quick Reference Guide

Critical Safety Issues (Immediate Stop)

  • Brakes: Pedal sinks, no resistance, grinding, leaks, grabbing
  • Steering: Excessive play (>75mm), difficulty steering, pulling
  • Oil Warning Light: Stop immediately, check for leaks
  • Loose Wheel Nuts: Zero tolerance - immediate stop
  • Red ABS Light: If persists after reset and 10mph check

🚨 EMERGENCY STOP PROCEDURES

🛑 Brake Failure

Symptoms: Pedal sinks to floor, no resistance, ineffective braking, grinding sounds, visible leaks, grabbing/shuddering, red ABS/EBS light

Action: STOP IMMEDIATELY → Switch off engine → Contact Engineering URGENT → Await assistance

🎯 Steering Problems

Symptoms: Excessive play (>75mm), difficulty steering, unusual noises, pulling to one side, visible damage, power steering leaks, stiff/unresponsive

Action: STOP SAFELY ASAP → Switch off engine → DO NOT attempt to drive → Contact Engineering

🛢️ Oil Warning Light

Risk: Immediate engine damage from loss of oil pressure

Action: STOP NOW → Switch off engine → Check for visible leaks → DO NOT restart → Contact Engineering

🔩 Loose Wheel Nuts

Risk: Catastrophic wheel detachment

Action: STOP IMMEDIATELY → DO NOT continue under any circumstances → Report to depot engineering manager, general manager AND engineering delivery director

🚨 Red ABS Light (Persistent)

Condition: Red ABS light remains on after reset and reaching 10mph

Action: STOP immediately → Contact Engineering URGENT → Do not continue
