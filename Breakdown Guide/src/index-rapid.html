🚨 EMERGENCY STOP PROCEDURES

🛑 Brake Failure

Pedal sinks to floor, no resistance, ineffective braking, grinding sounds, visible leaks, grabbing/shuddering

Action: Stop immediately, switch off engine, contact Engineering URGENT

🎯 Steering Issues

Excessive play (>75mm), difficulty steering, unusual noises, pulling to one side, visible damage, leaks

Action: Stop safely ASAP, switch off engine, DO NOT attempt to drive

🛢️ Oil Warning Light

Engine oil pressure warning - immediate engine damage risk

Action: Stop NOW, switch off engine, check for leaks, DO NOT restart

🔩 Loose Wheel Nuts

Zero tolerance - wheel detachment danger

Action: STOP immediately, DO NOT continue, contact ALL management levels

🚨 Red ABS Light Persistent

Red ABS light remains on after reset and 10mph check

Action: Stop immediately, contact Engineering URGENT
